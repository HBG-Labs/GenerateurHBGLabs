## ASSET GAP

This is the strongest cross-category quality ceiling. Public cases repeatedly
combine illustration, UI, photography, texture, product render and true 3D.
P3.2 can animate approved layers but cannot create or understand them. More
MotionPhrases alone will not solve this.

A future Procedural Asset Grammar should be data-only, provider-free initially,
reuse P1 asset/SVG/path security, and expose coherent layered vector/UI/data
parts. External media remains behind AssetIntent and approved binding.

## COMPOSITION GAP

Professional results frequently come from coordinated compositions rather than
single primitives. Eleven TechniqueCompositions are normalized, including UI
depth/camera focus, mixed-media coherence, brand motion recipes, data-to-story,
product feature metaphors and complexity/breath/payoff arcs.

P3.2 can execute useful subsets, but the future Director needs to select these
as wholes rather than stack isolated patterns.

## ART DIRECTION GAP

Palette coherence, scale hierarchy, negative space, motif evolution, material
contrast, crop and restraint remain selection problems. They are not repaired by
adding a low-level primitive. They belong principally to Visual Director policy
plus asset/style tokens.

## VISUAL DIRECTOR GAP

The missing high-value layer is sequence-level choice:

- when to transform versus cut;
- when to remain still;
- which motif persists;
- how intensity and density evolve;
- which TechniqueComposition fits the semantic role;
- which asset class is available and approved;
- how to avoid repetitive layouts and effect soup.

This directly supports beginning P3.3 with closed Structured Output and local
VisualPreflight.

## RENDERER GAP

- complete glyph/run-driven mask transformation;
- deterministic texture/matte/material allowlist;
- general overlapping inter-scene compositor (not yet justified for P1);
- richer but still controlled compatible vector morph;
- true 3D compositing boundary in a future separate system.

No renderer change is authorized by this report.

## 3D GAP

Eight retained references provide true-3D or cinematic-depth evidence. Product
mechanism reveals, real material/light, perspective camera, environments and
simulation require a future `3DScenePlan` and controlled renderer. Many depth,
portal and parallax effects remain honest 2.5D and should not force Blender.

## AUDIO GAP

Several creator pages explicitly mention music, SFX or speech alignment.
`SOUND_MOTION_COUPLING` remains a future AudioPlan/Sound Director concern.
Existing deterministic sync anchors are the correct handoff; no audio system was
implemented.

Marginal language discovery therefore decreased after wave F. Additional

## P3.3 READINESS

**READY**.

The expanded evidence does not demonstrate one absent engine primitive that
would fundamentally limit almost every Visual Director output. P3.2 already has
a substantial closed vocabulary. The largest repeated gaps are asset/material
richness and intelligent sequence selection; the latter is exactly P3.3's role.

Readiness constraints:

- no unsupported ID may be exposed;
- Structured Output plus local validation remains mandatory;
- general compositor/path-follow/arbitrary morph/per-cluster/true-3D stay out;
- real assets must already be approved and bound;
- P3.3 must not claim final premium/showreel parity;
- Procedural Asset Grammar remains Priority A parallel/next work.

## RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP

After explicit human authorization, start P3.3 as a provider-agnostic Visual
Director contract and deterministic offline fixture first. It should choose
SequenceStrategy, visual focus, TechniqueComposition, registered patterns,
phrases, bridges, camera/depth, motif, restraint and pacing. No provider is
started by P3.2.5.

In parallel or immediately after, authorize a separate Procedural Asset Grammar
foundation. Do not fold that work into P1 or ask the Director to invent assets.
