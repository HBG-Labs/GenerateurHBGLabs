# Procedural icon catalog

> P3.3.6 étend le registre interne à vingt rôles cohérents. Voir [Premium Procedural Design Catalog](./premium-procedural-design-catalog.md). Les entrées P3.3.5 ci-dessous restent historiques et inchangées.

The internal allowlist contains `CHECK`, `SPARK`, `CLOCK`, `TREND`, `GRID`, `ARROW`, `FOCUS` and `BELL`, each versioned `1.0.0`.

Icons are normalized internal paths or composed primitives. The active Product/App reference uses the trend/check family in card and chart contexts. Icon style is kept coherent at sequence level; external packs, raw SVG, URLs and user-supplied path commands are not accepted.

The catalog is intentionally small. Its role is to prove a secure, addressable icon boundary, not to replace a future curated illustration or brand-icon library.
